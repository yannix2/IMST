## Decisions this plan builds on (owner, 2026-11-06)

- **Consent:** only non-identifiable shots of children (backs, hands, wide shots, staff, adults). Close-up portraits are never used (e.g. "Elections Primary Portraits").
- **Colours:** turquoise leads on a white page (buttons, highlights, icons, active states, wash bands); navy for headings, footer, a few deep plates and the hero veil. Text on turquoise is night `#0B2A47` (5.36:1); white never sits on `#00ADBB` (2.73:1).
- **Hero:** full-screen muted looping video behind the text, navy veil, pause button, poster first; reduced motion or Save-Data shows the poster only.
- **Hero footage:** Claude picks 4 to 8 non-identifiable shots from contact sheets; the owner approves before use.
- **Cambridge:** temporary high-quality logo, marked placeholder; the official toolkit replaces it later.
- **Foundation logos:** turkiyemaarif.org/corporate-identity (horizontal/vertical PNG, "Dişi" = reversed for dark grounds).
- **Flip cards:** school levels, and school life & events. Click flips; no hover flip.
- **Journey:** our school in Tunisia; dated old-site events plus `[to confirm]` slots (dev only).
- **Figures:** turkiyemaarif.org/dunyada-maarif, read 2026-11-06: 66 countries, 604 institutions, 516 schools, 75,000+ students, 2 universities, 55 dormitories (replaces 64).
- **Globe:** a dot per country, arcs from Istanbul, Tunis highlighted, searchable country list beside it.
- **Galleries (21st.dev):** Card Fan Carousel = Life at Maarif; Circular Gallery = Cambridge photos; designali gallery = Our journey. Free plan: 2 downloads per day.
